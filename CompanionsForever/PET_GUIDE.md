## Farming, equipment and the collection log

**XP Treats** are new bag drops, separate from the food used for feeding shown beside your tokens. Each eligible enemy has an independent 2% chance to drop one. Common / Uncommon / Rare / Epic / Legendary treats grant **25 / 50 / 100 / 200 / 400 XP** respectively; their rarity weights are 60% / 25% / 10% / 4% / 1%. Right-click or choose **Use treat** to give one to the equipped living pet. XP is flat, without gear or talent multipliers, and stops at the pet's current level cap. Already-capped pets keep the treat. Treats cannot be used during a battle or trade.

Coin pouches and XP treats stack to **30 per type and rarity**. The bag shows the count; using an item consumes only one unit. New drops and mail can join a matching partial stack even when every bag slot is occupied. Drag matching partial stacks together to combine them up to 30; any remainder stays in its original slot. Stack contents and positions survive reloads. Trades offer individually tracked units, and both players must use the updated addon. Ordinary gear and adoption/equipment containers remain separate items.

The Equipment bag keeps fixed item positions, including empty spaces after sales or equipping, across reloads. New items fill empty unlocked slots. Drag an item to an empty bag slot to move it, or onto another bag item to swap their positions. Drag gear onto its matching equipment slot to equip it; the old gear returns to the source bag slot. You can also drag worn gear into an empty bag slot, or onto matching loose gear to exchange them. Filters dim other items without moving them. Escape, right-click while holding an item, or dropping outside cancels. Ordinary right-click equip/open and Shift-click multi-sale still work. Locked slots and incompatible equipment reject drops. Rarity colors remain on bag and equipped-icon frames while the selection highlight appears inside them.

Open **Collection log** or **/cfpets collection** to browse the allowed roster, filter collected/missing/farmable entries, and see NPC and coordinate hints. Unsupported expansion entries are hidden and cannot drop. Previous unsupported pets and waiting finds are archived without losing discovery history; their equipment returns to the bag even if it temporarily exceeds capacity. Pet IDs remain unchanged. A matching model is not evidence of a killable world spawn.

Solo kills on listed NPCs have a **5% base pet drop chance**, increased by pet-drop talents. In a party, each observed creature kill has one shared **5% result**: every nearby addon user with stable space receives a copy when it succeeds. Pet-drop talents do not increase that shared rate. Rarity rolls individually. Raids cannot farm these pets. All members need this update and must receive a readable kill event; hidden creature identity cannot identify a specific pet. A separate **3% equipment roll** applies to green-through-red NPC kills with an observed enemy level and bag space. Pet farming has no player-level or active-companion requirement. Gear rolls do not require a companion and continue after the 12-XP-per-minute cap. Rates are independent per kill, with no guaranteed drop after a fixed number. Duplicate events are ignored, including recent kills across reloads.

Equipment rarity follows the killed enemy's level. Relative rarity weights are preserved within the allowed range; Lucky Find cannot exceed its ceiling. Shop equipment boxes use their own rarity rules. Existing gear is unchanged.

Recognized skull world bosses and boss-unit frames use level 63 for addon loot eligibility when no numeric level is available. Gray equipment gives half of the Common stat bonus and sells for 1 token; it never applies to pet quality.

Pet and equipment drops roll Common **50%**, Uncommon **30%**, Rare **15%**, Epic **4%**, Legendary **1%**. Duplicate pets can drop. Solo pet finds during a tower fight, or while the stable is full, wait in the collection log (up to 100). Party finds require stable space at the time of the kill; a full stable receives no copy. Claiming them needs room in the stable and no active battle. A full waiting list suspends further pet drops until space is available.

Use the tabs in the main window to switch sections. The Collection tab supports name search, mouse-wheel scrolling, a scrollbar and All/Collected/Undiscovered filters. Missing portraits are gray; collected portraits show color and rarity.

Open **Equipment** or **/cfpets gear**. Every pet has exactly five slots: **Weapon**, **Head**, **Body**, **Collar** and **Trinket**. Regular and hard-mode item types grant health, attack, armor or speed; their base bonuses multiply by rarity rank (1–5). Equipment does not change the model's appearance. Click Equip to move one item onto the active pet, or click its equipped slot to remove it. An item belongs to at most one pet. Changing gear is blocked during battle, and never heals the pet's current health percentage. The equipment bag is an 8 by 8 grid with 20 free slots for unequipped items and unopened crates; further equipment drops stop when the unlocked slots are full. Locked slots show only a padlock. Click one to select it, then click Buy at the bottom to purchase it permanently. The price appears in the selected-slot details and Buy button; selecting a slot never spends tokens. Every lock shares the current price: 1, 2, 4, 7, 11, 16... tokens. The price increase grows by one per purchase; the final slot costs 947 tokens, and all 44 extra slots cost 14,234 tokens total. Hover an icon for stats, ownership and sale value. Select an unequipped item and click Sell to receive 2/5/12/30/75 tokens for Common/Uncommon/Rare/Epic/Legendary gear. Existing items are preserved. On the first upgrade, enough slots are unlocked for existing loose items (up to 64); any further legacy overflow remains accessible on extra pages. These migrated extra slots count toward the next unlock price.

Animated notifications show item/pet finds and crate opening/reveal. XP and level-up effects appear around the pet inside Camp or Tower; both tower views have XP bars, a level arrow and the native gold level-up model. Reduced Motion keeps static text cues. These are virtual addon rewards, never real bag items, gold or server loot.

The log retains discoveries after death or retirement and migrates old pets as **Existing collection** without inventing an unlock timeline. New finds keep a bounded local history. This is not anti-cheat proof; local saves can be edited. Multiplayer equipment inspection, full collection inspection, ignore/review tools and PvP are later work.

## Talents and retirement

Pets start with a level-10 cap. Every third trainer level raises it by 10: trainer levels 3, 6 and 9 unlock pet caps 20, 30 and 40, continuing to cap 100 at trainer level 27. Trainer levels 28–40 provide further talent points. Existing pets above the current cap keep their level but earn no further XP until the cap exceeds it. XP is not banked at cap.

Retire a living or dead pet of any level in Talents, or use Retire beside its name in Camp. Review the full reward and confirm before removal. Each pet level gives 1 reward point; Common/Uncommon/Rare/Epic/Legendary add 1/3/5/7/10 points. Every 10 pet levels adds 25% to the level portion (+25% at 10, +50% at 20, through +250% at 100). Each reward point converts to 10 talent XP. Mentor boosts the combined reward, then dead pets receive a 50% penalty; only the final XP total is rounded down. A living level-10 Common gives 135 XP; a dead one gives 67 XP without Mentor. Death does not automatically retire a pet. Retirement permanently removes that pet, returns its gear, and retains the collection record. Each trainer level grants one point. The next level requires `100 + 50 × current level + 4 × max(0, current level - 2)^2` XP: the first three points cost 450 total, then progress slows. The gentler XP curve keeps existing allocations and total earned XP. Reloading recalculates mastery against the lower costs; any additional levels grant unspent points. No reset is needed. Pet caps 60, 80 and 100 now require 9,350, 21,036 and 39,850 total training XP; full 40-point mastery requires 113,300. With living Common pets trained to each available cap and no Mentor bonus, these correspond to about 17, 24, 31 and 52 cumulative retirements. These are retirement estimates, not guarantees of playtime or tower victories.

Endurance: Vitality (5 × 1% health), Thick Hide (2 × 0.5 armor points), Resilience (3 × 2% less poison/burn), Steady Income (3 × 10% online tokens). Expertise: Power (4 × 1% attack), Mentor (2 × 2% retirement XP), Tower Bounty (2 × 5% victory tokens), Lucky Find (5 × 0.3 percentage points to top crate rarity; 0.3% chance per rank to upgrade world drops one tier). Exploration: Agility (3 × 1% speed), Tracker (2 × 0.75 percentage points solo pet drops), Herbalist (4 × 0.25 percentage points herb drops), Scavenger (5 × 0.3 percentage points gear drops). Fractional token bonuses carry between rewards. Lucky Find never exceeds Legendary or the enemy gear bracket.

## Stats and abilities

Every pet has max HP, attack, armor (percentage damage reduction), and speed. These depend on its creature family, rarity and level; reopening the addon never rerolls them. Higher speed acts first; ties favor the pet. Common and Uncommon pets only have basic attacks, guard and supplies. Rare pets receive one random special; Epic and Legendary pets receive two distinct specials. They share a three-round cooldown. Rolls persist through reloads; existing pets get assigned once. Family stats remain separate specialties, shown in Camp and collection tooltips. Damage abilities fire automatically; healing abilities require the Heal button:

The large window lays out each combat stat separately, including hit chance, miss chance and XP. Hit chance is `92 + floor((attacker speed - defender speed) / 3)` percent, clamped to 75–97%. Both pet attacks and enemy attacks/spells can miss. Each cleave target rolls separately. A missed drain heals for zero; Wild Growth's fixed heal and Iron Hide's protection still work. Healing herbs and guard cannot miss. Tower hit chance uses the current enemy; camp and inspection show the 92% equal-speed baseline.

## Tower and income

Version 0.2.0 strengthens the tower as you progress. The combat level is `floor + floor(max(0, pet level - floor - 2) x 0.8)`. The health budget gains another `0.6% x (floor - 1)` and attack gains `0.15% x (floor - 1)` before elite modifiers. Replays use the same scaling but retain reduced rewards. Battle-start stats stay fixed even if a world kill levels your pet during the fight. Preview enemies show the same stats used when you enter.

From floor 5, each enemy receives one spell, rotating between Venom, Firebolt, Frost Nova and Crippling Hex. Ordinary enemies cast every five rounds; elite leaders every four, staggered by enemy slot. A cast replaces the regular/heavy attack and deals 65% attack damage on hit, then applies its effect unless you guard:

| Effect | Duration | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Poison | 3 pet turns | 18% of caster attack per tick, reduced by armor |
| Burn | 2 pet turns | 28% of caster attack per tick, reduced by armor |
| Frozen | 1 pet turn | Offensive action skipped; manual healing actions remain available |
| Slowed | 3 pet turns | Turn speed and attack frequency reduced by 35% |

Effects of the same kind refresh without stacking damage. Poison and burn can coexist and tick before the pet acts; healing applies immediately before later ticks. Guard prevents new effects, but does not cleanse existing ones. Effects clear when combat ends. The stats panel and compact tower show remaining pet turns; hover enemy portraits for their spell and combat stats.

Starting a fight requires at least 40 health, 9 energy and 15 food. Both tower views show what is missing next to the Fight controls; hover Fight for recovery instructions. Rest in Camp to restore energy, feed for food, and use herbs for health. Starting a fight spends 9 energy and 5 food.

Each pet starts with only floor 1 open. Defeat that floor to unlock the next, up to 100. Floors 1–33 have one opponent, 34–66 have two, and 67–100 have three. Every tenth floor (10 through 100) has an elite leader: its encounter gets 55% more health and 25% more attack than the ordinary formula for that floor. The leader has 3 extra armor and a 185% heavy attack every third round (ordinary enemies use 160%). Later elite floors include one or two weaker supporting enemies. Health and attack are split across the group rather than multiplied by its size, with the elite receiving the largest share. The elite is larger and has a gold dragon around its portrait and an ELITE label. Tower unit frames use the client's target-frame artwork, green health bars, percentage and current HP, round portraits and level badges (tower floor for enemies). Hover for full names and current / maximum HP. Each living actor gets a speed-ordered turn. The battle display animates those turns in sequence.

Choose Balanced, Aggressive, Defensive or Specialist beside the tower controls. Attacks, guards and automatic specials follow that style. Click **Heal** for instant herb healing without spending an attack turn; when herbs run out, the button can purchase one at the displayed combat price. Full-health pets do not consume supplies.

Healing and control specials have separate buttons. Left-click queues one for the pet's next action; click again to cancel. Right-click toggles automatic use, saved for that pet. Auto-use follows the fighting style and shared cooldown. Healing specials wait until health falls below 85%; they never spend herbs or tokens. Ordinary attack and defense specials remain automatic. Frost Nova and Hammer of Justice can interrupt an opponent, followed by brief control immunity. Existing pets keep their learned abilities and rolls.

Whenever a tower floor contains multiple enemies, choose Lowest HP, Highest HP, In Order or Random. Tactics can change during battle; fighting styles are locked until it ends. **Pause** lets you plan. Clearing a floor selects the next one but does not start it. Hidden tower views pause combat; reloading retreats. **Tower defeat is permanent death. Hunger cannot kill a pet.**

- Starting wallet: 100 tokens, 2 food and 2 herbs for fresh characters.
- Time online: 2 tokens per five minutes, even with no living or equipped pet. Fresh characters start with 100 tokens, 2 food and 2 herbs. Loading gaps are capped; offline time earns nothing.
- First floor clear: `12 + floor(floor number / 5)` tokens, plus 10 for regular elite floors or 20 for rare elites. XP is `18 + 3 × floor number`.
- Repeated floor: 1 token and 40% of the first-clear XP.
- Eligible green-through-red NPC kills: an equal-chance roll of 0 or 1 token for normal enemies, or 0, 1 or 2 for known elites (including rare elites and bosses). Tokens do not need bag space or an active pet and are independent of the 12-XP-per-minute cap. Hidden dungeon enemies use the normal roll unless elite status was observed. Boss completion rewards remain separate.
- Store prices: food 2 tokens; healing herbs 4. Purchases add stock without using it. Prepare between floors rather than entering injured. The store is available in the compass and large pet view.
- Eligible personal and nearby group kills: green 1 pet XP, yellow 2-3, orange 3-4, red 4-5. Elites and rare elites add 1 XP before the cap. All share a 12 XP per minute cap, with the final reward reduced to fit. Gray targets give no XP. Rewards work even when the player earns no XP; observation and repeat-target limits still apply.

Automated battle checks exercise the original 100 species across five rarities with seeded random actions and manual healing requests. Separate tests cover misses, spells, timing, and progression. Survival is not guaranteed; gear, talents, supplies and attentive healing matter.

Kill XP uses `PARTY_KILL` events for your character, combat pet or nearby party/raid members and their pets. Target, focus, mouseover, nameplates, party/raid targets and combat-pet targets supply recent level observations. A corpse's temporary zero/nil level keeps its last positive observation for up to 60 seconds. Equal-level and up-to-four-level-lower targets do not require optional gray-range APIs; the five-level-lower boundary requires a readable supported gray range. Unknown, ordinary skull, stale or known out-of-range targets are rejected. Readable duplicate targets remain limited to once per five minutes, with 6 combat XP per minute and no XP for dead companions or pets at the current unlocked cap. Player XP events are never used, so level-capped characters can still progress pets.

In PvE dungeons and raids, a confirmed party kill with restricted identity, level or party-range details gives **1 XP** instead. It shares the same **12 XP per minute** budget with normal kill rewards and uses the normal mini portrait XP animation. Hidden details cannot reliably exclude gray mobs, add elite bonuses or deduplicate hidden victim identities. Readable gray enemies still give no XP. This fallback does not apply in the open world, battlegrounds or arenas. Use `/cfpets xp` to see why the last kill did or did not award XP.

**Dungeon equipment:** the normal **3% base chance** continues even after the XP cap, without a living pet, or with a pet at its level cap. If a dungeon enemy's level cannot be read, gear rarity uses your readable character level instead; a known enemy level always takes precedence. This equipment exception is for five-player dungeons only, not raids, the open world or PvP. Readable gray enemies are excluded; hidden gray enemies cannot be identified. Fallback items record a player-derived level, never a guessed NPC level. Hidden identities do not award specific creature pets. Bag limits, rarity weights, gear-drop talents and herb rolls still apply.

Both paths share one saved reward claim. Hidden boss names do not block dungeon rewards when the encounter ID is readable. Wipes, outdoor elites, unpaired encounter-end events and changing instances between start/end do not count.

## Food and needs

Each food restores 35 hunger points and 10% maximum health, capped at full, outside tower combat. Food primarily comes from the token store. First-time tower clears have a 10% chance for one food; replaying a completed floor has no food roll. Eligible personal NPC killing blows have a 1% chance for one food, capped at one success per 30 minutes with a saved cooldown. PvP, combat-pet killing blows, gray/out-of-range or unreadable targets do not drop food. A living equipped pet is required; level-100 pets can still get food. Existing repeat-target checks apply. These are virtual companion supplies, not WoW loot.


- Food below 25 shows a red hunger pulse and hunger mark in the mini portrait and camp views. Reduced motion keeps the warning steady.
- Food below 10, including zero, drains health and energy by one point per minute outside battle, stopping at 1. Rest cannot counter starvation. Feed food to stop the drain; use herbs to restore health.
- Energy below 15 prevents another battle. With food at least 10 it recovers naturally, faster while resting. Rest restores energy first, then heals 1 actual HP every 5 seconds at full energy, displays sleeping Zs in the mini portrait and camp, and ends when both health and energy are full. Zero energy does not kill a pet or interrupt a current fight.
- Stabled and offline pets do not lose needs.

## Recovery, care and trainer racials

### Easier trade invitations (1.1.25)

Select a player in Inspect and click **Request trade**. An incoming invitation has **Join trade**, **View offer** and **Decline** buttons, plus a sound and chat alert. It appears even with the addon windows hidden. The Mini alert opens Trade directly; the requesting player also stays visible in Inspect while the request is pending.

Joining opens the trade without confirming it. If you already prepared an offer, review it first. Both players still review the items and confirm after the three-second countdown. Requests expire after two minutes. Declining or expiry tells the sender to cancel; an offline sender may need to reconnect before held items return. Two simultaneous requests between the same players become one trade.


## Appearance and accessibility

Normal and Hard Mode are included in the same addon. Clearing normal floor 100 plays the unlock reveal and enables the optional purple theme. Settings lets you toggle that theme and the unlock animation separately, or replay the reveal after unlocking. High Contrast takes priority over the purple theme and uses solid dark panels, white text, distinct tabs, readable talent ranks and selection markers, and clear XP tracks. Unlock banners and reward reveals gain solid dark backings; flashes are suppressed. Reduced Motion also keeps the unlock reveal still and removes animated reward movement. These settings do not change progression or reward odds.

Collection includes an Abilities spellbook. Unfound abilities are gray; found abilities have explanatory tooltips. Equipped ornate portrait frames keep their square proportions.


## Companion milestone achievements

Under Collection > Achievements > Companions, earn token rewards for these milestones:

| Category | Milestones | Token rewards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sparkly discoveries | 1 / 3 / 5 different species | 250 / 500 / 1,000 |
| Food or XP treats consumed | 10 / 50 / 250 | 25 / 75 / 250 |
| Abilities discovered | 5 / 10 / 20 different abilities | 75 / 150 / 300 |
| Gold stat | First perfect 100/100 base stat | 150 |

Food and XP Treats both count when consumed, starting with 1.1.28. Saved collection and spellbook discoveries count immediately. Duplicate Sparkly species or abilities count once. A gold stat must be a perfect base health, attack, armor or speed roll; silver rolls, ability rolls and equipment bonuses do not qualify. Owned pets, memorials, Mail and outgoing trade escrow are checked on reload. Each reward is claimed once per shared realm adventure, and discoveries remain after retirement or trading.


## Party gold and Hard Mode discoveries

Earn 2 extra companion gold per minute for each other connected party member with the addon, up to 8 per minute. These are addon tokens, not native WoW gold. Raid members and guild-only contacts do not count. Recent addon presence or sharing confirms membership. The bonus is flat; incomplete minutes reset on leaving, loss of confirmation or UI reload. There is no offline catch-up.

After Hard Mode unlock, new Rare/Epic/Legendary companions can roll 18 additional abilities. Existing pets keep their abilities. The new abilities use the existing slots, rolled strength and shared three-turn cooldown. They remain hidden in the ability book until discovered. Hard Tower enemies can use them; Normal enemies do not. New trades require updated clients; older Inspect requests omit unsupported pets.

Fight/Replay shows Wait through the finishing attack and rewards, then unlocks when the tower advances. Floor 100 unlocks after rewards without changing modes.

Lucky Find is now the final Expertise node, after Tower Bounty. Scavenger is the final Exploration node, after Herbalist, and gives +0.3 percentage points to eligible equipment drops per rank (3% becomes 4.5% at 5/5). Expertise and Exploration allocations are refunded once; earned XP, levels and all talent points are kept. Tracker gives +0.75 percentage points per rank to solo farmable pet drops: 5%, 5.75%, 6.5%; party drops stay 5%.
